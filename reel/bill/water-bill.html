Notice of your rights

Objection. You may object to the drinking water charges in this statement within one month of receiving it. Please send your objection in writing to Stadtwerke Musterstadt GmbH, Am Wasserwerk 1, 12345 Musterstadt, or by e-mail to service@stadtwerke-musterstadt.example, stating your customer number and the reason for your objection.

Wastewater fees. The sewage and rainwater fees are levied on behalf of the City of Musterstadt under its wastewater fee statute. An objection against these fees may be lodged within one month of notification, in writing or for the record, with the City of Musterstadt, Civil Engineering Department, Rathausplatz 1, 12345 Musterstadt. An objection does not suspend the obligation to pay.

Errors in the statement. If you believe a meter reading or calculation is wrong, you may request a check of the meter by an accredited testing body. If the meter is found to exceed the permitted error limits, the costs of the check are borne by us and the statement will be corrected.

Late payment. If the amount due is not received by the due date, we may charge a reminder fee of 2.50 € and default interest at the statutory rate. Repeated non-payment may lead to a restriction of supply after prior written notice.

Consumer arbitration. For disputes about this contract you may contact the consumer arbitration board for water supply, Musterstraße 10, 12345 Musterstadt. We are willing to take part in arbitration proceedings.

Data protection. We process your personal data to perform your supply contract. Details are available from our data protection officer at the address above.
